The registered assets of the fingerprinting track were also watermarked with one openly licensed model per modality from the watermarking track, and the marked copies went through the fingerprinting track's transformations (Section~\ref{sec:fp-method}). This measures, on identical media and attacks, the coverage of each family's decision rule and their complementarity. The union of their successes describes combined coverage; a deployed watermark-first cascade would also need to handle incorrect and ambiguous keys.
