\section{Changes in Version 1.1}
\label{sec:v11}

Version 1.1 responds to two pieces of feedback on version 1.0 (arXiv:2610.04151v1). Every v1.1 number below comes from a rerun by the authors, on an Apple M5 Pro rather than the cloud hosts of version 1.0, with the scripts and records in the \texttt{rerun-2026-11} directories of the release.

\paragraph{ISCC.} The ISCC Foundation reproduced our ISCC results and traced the watermark drift and the transfer-attack failures to two implementation issues, which it fixed: up to iscc-sdk 0.9.6 the border trim removed only borders of exactly one colour, and up to iscc-core 1.4.0 bits 65 to 256 of the Image-Code came from shifted copies of the first DCT block instead of the quadrants ISO 24138 specifies. We reran the drift measurement of Section~\ref{sec:together} with both versions on the same \val{v11:iscc/0.9.5/n} product photos, marked again with TrustMark-Q at strength 1. With the versions of version 1.0 (iscc-sdk 0.9.5, iscc-core 1.4.0) the rerun reproduces the recorded drift (64-bit \val{v11:iscc/0.9.5/ISCC-Image-64/drift/pct}\% against \val{fp:wm/image/ISCC-Image-64/drift/pct}\% recorded; 256-bit \val{v11:iscc/0.9.5/ISCC-Image-256/drift/pct}\% against \val{fp:wm/image/ISCC-Image-256/drift/pct}\%). With iscc-sdk 0.9.7, iscc-core 1.5.0 and iscc-lib 0.7.0 the drift falls to \val{v11:iscc/0.9.7/ISCC-Image-64/drift/pct}\% (\val{v11:iscc/0.9.7/ISCC-Image-64/drift/k} of \val{v11:iscc/0.9.7/n}) and \val{v11:iscc/0.9.7/ISCC-Image-256/drift/pct}\% (\val{v11:iscc/0.9.7/ISCC-Image-256/drift/k} of \val{v11:iscc/0.9.7/n}). The 64-bit threshold is the same score (\val{v11:iscc/0.9.7/ISCC-Image-64/threshold}) in both versions, so its drop is a property of the code and not of recalibration. We also reran the ISCC image track at full scale with iscc-sdk 0.9.5, on the same ABO registry and queries without DISC21, to check that our host reproduces the recorded results before measuring the update: detection at pair-level $10^{-7}$ is \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.5/ISCC-Image-64/tpr7/pct}\% (64-bit) and \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.5/ISCC-Image-256/tpr7/pct}\% (256-bit), Recall@1 \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.5/ISCC-Image-64/r1/pct}\% and \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.5/ISCC-Image-256/r1/pct}\%, with every calibrated threshold equal to the recorded one; the only differences are confined to the transformations that render text or graphics (text, meme and the re-capture chain), whose font rendering differs between the two hosts, and they change no pooled rate at the reported precision; for example, the held-out false-match rate differs by one or two of 26{,}820 negative queries. Without near-duplicate cleanup the 256-bit code detects \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.5/ISCC-Image-256/nodedup/q01/pct}\% at the query-level 1\% threshold, as recorded. With the update, the same full-scale run detects \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.7/ISCC-Image-64/tpr7/pct}\% (64-bit) and \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.7/ISCC-Image-256/tpr7/pct}\% (256-bit) of attacked copies at $10^{-7}$, against PDQ's \val{fp:image/PDQ/TPR@pair@1e-07/pct}\%, with Recall@1 \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.7/ISCC-Image-64/r1/pct}\% and \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.7/ISCC-Image-256/r1/pct}\%. The largest change is WebP at quality 50 (64-bit Recall@1 \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.5/ISCC-Image-64/att/webp50/pct}\% to \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.7/ISCC-Image-64/att/webp50/pct}\%); the crop keeping 70\% of each side moves from \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.5/ISCC-Image-64/att/crop70/pct}\% to \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.7/ISCC-Image-64/att/crop70/pct}\%, and additive noise falls from \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.5/ISCC-Image-64/att/noise0.05/pct}\% to \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.7/ISCC-Image-64/att/noise0.05/pct}\%. Without near-duplicate cleanup the 256-bit code now detects \val{v11:isccfull/0.9.7/ISCC-Image-256/nodedup/tpr7/pct}\% at $10^{-7}$. These runs exclude DISC21, so the DISC21 columns of Table~\ref{tab:fp-image} are not rerun. The tables and figures of Sections~\ref{sec:fp-res-image} to \ref{sec:together} keep the iscc-sdk 0.9.5 records. Not yet rerun with the update: the security track, the registry experiment with watermarked assets, and the bootstrap intervals and paired tests (Table~\ref{tab:v11}).

\paragraph{TrustMark.} Adobe pointed out that version 1.0 decoded TrustMark with its decoder alone and never ran the watermark-region detector that the trustmark package ships for its Q and P models. Every TrustMark number in version 1.0, including the blind false-positive rates of Section~\ref{sec:wm-res-fpr}, is therefore a decoder-only number. Version 1.1 adds the detect-first path as a separate configuration and keeps decoder-only for reproducibility; its results are listed in Table~\ref{tab:v11} as they become available.
