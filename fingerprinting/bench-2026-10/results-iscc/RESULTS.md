# ISCC rerun: results

Generated by `iscc/compare.py` from `results-gpu/` (the paper), `results-iscc/iscc-sdk-0.9.5/` (this rerun with the ISCC versions the paper ran: iscc-sdk 0.9.5, iscc-core 1.4.0, iscc-lib 0.6.0) and `results-iscc/iscc-sdk-0.9.7/` (the update: iscc-sdk 0.9.7, iscc-core 1.5.0, iscc-lib 0.7.0, iscc-sci 0.4.1). The update also runs the Semantic-Code, which the paper measured in its pilot only: ISCC-SCI-256 with the paper's own adapter, ISCC-SCI-64 as its first 64 bits, and ISCC-Combined-512 as ISCC-SCI-256 followed by ISCC-Image-256, compared as one code. Each method has its own calibrated thresholds. Rates in percent. PDQ, DINOv2-B, ISC21-1st are the paper's records, for scale.

## The rerun reproduces the paper

- ISCC-Image-64, image track: identical to the paper's record: every figure, threshold and score histogram, up to float32 rounding.
- ISCC-Image-256, image track: identical to the paper's record: every figure, threshold and score histogram, up to float32 rounding.
- ISCC-Image-64, watermark track: the paper records 62.0 % of marked photos past the threshold, this rerun 62.3 %; whether the unattacked marked copy finds its unmarked original agrees for 299 of 300 photos.
- ISCC-Image-256, watermark track: the paper records 56.7 % of marked photos past the threshold, this rerun 56.7 %; whether the unattacked marked copy finds its unmarked original agrees for 299 of 300 photos.
